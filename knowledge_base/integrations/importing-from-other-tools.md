# Importing from Trello, Asana and Jira

You can import under Settings › Data › Import. Trello and Asana can be imported directly by connecting your account, including boards, lists, cards, assignees and attachments. Jira can be imported from a CSV export only; issue links and worklogs are not imported. Attachments are limited to 100 MB per file during import; larger files are skipped and listed in the import report. An import can take up to a few hours for large boards and you receive an email when it is complete.
