# Google Calendar sync

Two-way Google Calendar sync is available on the Team plan and above. Connect it under Profile › Integrations › Google Calendar. Tasks with a due date appear in a dedicated "Northwind" calendar; moving the event in Google Calendar updates the due date, and vice versa. Sync runs every 5 minutes. Only your own tasks are synced. Disconnecting removes the Northwind calendar from Google but does not change any tasks.
