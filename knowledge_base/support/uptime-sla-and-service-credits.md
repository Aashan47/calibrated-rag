# Uptime SLA and service credits

Business and Enterprise plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment. If availability in a calendar month falls below 99.9% you can claim a credit of 10% of that month's fee; below 99.0%, 25%; below 95.0%, 50%. Claims must be submitted to support@northwind.app within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to a future invoice. Scheduled maintenance is excluded from the calculation; it is announced at least 72 hours in advance and takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC.
