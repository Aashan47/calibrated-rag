# Support hours and response times

Free plan: community forum and email support, with a first response within 2 business days. Team plan: email support with a first response within 1 business day. Business plan: email and live chat (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time) with a first response within 8 business hours. Enterprise plan: 24/7 email and chat, a dedicated account manager, and a 1-hour first response for priority-1 incidents. Phone support is available on the Enterprise plan only.
