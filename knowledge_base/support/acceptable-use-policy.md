# Acceptable use policy

You may not use Northwind to send unsolicited messages, store or share content that is illegal or infringes someone else's rights, attempt to access other customers' data, or run automated traffic that degrades the service for others. Workspaces that violate the policy receive an email warning and 7 days to remedy the issue before the workspace is suspended. Suspended workspaces can export their data for 30 days before deletion.
