# Roles and permissions

There are four roles. The Owner is the single person who can transfer ownership, delete the workspace and change the plan; the Owner can also do everything an Admin can. Admins manage members, roles, integrations and workspace security settings. Members can create and edit projects and tasks they have access to. Guests can only see the projects they have been invited to and cannot see the member directory. Roles are changed under Settings › Members by the Owner or an Admin; ownership transfer is under Settings › General and requires the new owner to accept.
