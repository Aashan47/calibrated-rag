# Resetting your password

Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. The reset link in the email is valid for 1 hour and can be used once. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the address your account was created with; for security reasons we do not say whether an address is registered. If your workspace enforces single sign-on, your password is managed by your identity provider and must be reset there instead.
