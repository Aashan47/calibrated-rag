# Single sign-on (SAML SSO)

SAML 2.0 single sign-on is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. We support Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), Google Workspace and any SAML 2.0 identity provider. The workspace Owner configures it under Settings › Security › Single sign-on by uploading the identity provider metadata. The "Enforce SSO" option requires every member to sign in through the identity provider and disables password sign-in for them. Automatic user provisioning and deprovisioning via SCIM is available on the Enterprise plan only.
