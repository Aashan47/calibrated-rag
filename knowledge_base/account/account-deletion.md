# Deleting your account

You can delete your personal account under Profile › Delete account. Deletion takes effect after a 7-day grace period, during which signing in again cancels the request. If you own a workspace you must transfer ownership first or delete the workspace; the request is blocked until you do. Content you created in shared workspaces (tasks, comments, files) remains in those workspaces and is attributed to "Deleted user". After the grace period your profile, personal settings and API tokens are permanently removed.
