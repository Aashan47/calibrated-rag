# File upload and storage limits

The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 25 MB on the Free plan, 250 MB on Team and 5 GB on Business and Enterprise. Total storage is 2 GB on Free, 100 GB per workspace on Team, and unlimited on Business and Enterprise. When a workspace reaches its storage limit, existing files stay available but new uploads fail with the message "Storage limit reached" until files are deleted or the plan is upgraded.
