# Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express for all paid plans. Bank transfer (ACH in the United States, SEPA in Europe) is available for Business and Enterprise workspaces on annual billing when the invoice total is $5,000 or more. Enterprise customers can request net-30 payment terms. We do not accept PayPal, cryptocurrency or prepaid gift cards. Cards are charged automatically on the renewal date; if a charge fails we retry on days 3, 7 and 14 and email the billing contact each time before the workspace is moved to read-only.
