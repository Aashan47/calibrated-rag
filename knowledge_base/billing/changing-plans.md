# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

Upgrades take effect immediately. The price difference for the remaining part of the billing period is charged right away, prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, so you keep the features you paid for until then. If a downgrade leaves the workspace over the new plan's member or storage limit, members above the limit become read-only and new uploads are blocked until the workspace is back within the limit. Only the workspace Owner or a Billing admin can change the plan, under Settings › Billing › Plans.
