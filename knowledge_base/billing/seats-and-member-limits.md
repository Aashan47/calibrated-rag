# Seats, members and guests

A seat is counted for every active member of the workspace. Guests do not use seats: each paid seat includes up to 3 free guests, who can only see the projects they are invited to. Deactivating a member frees their seat immediately and the seat can be reassigned the same day. On monthly billing, seat changes are reflected on the next invoice. On annual billing, added seats are charged as a prorated addendum for the remainder of the term, and removed seats are credited to the workspace balance rather than refunded.
