# Nonprofit and education discounts

Registered nonprofits receive 50% off the Team and Business plans on annual billing. Accredited schools, colleges and universities also receive 50% off for workspaces used for teaching or academic research. To apply, email billing@northwind.app with proof of status (for example a 501(c)(3) determination letter or an institutional email address); approval usually takes 2 business days. Discounts are applied to the whole workspace, cannot be combined with other promotions, and are reviewed at each annual renewal.
