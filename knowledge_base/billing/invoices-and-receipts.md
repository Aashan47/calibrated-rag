# Invoices and receipts

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month for monthly plans and on the renewal date for annual plans. They are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded as PDF by the Owner or any Billing admin under Settings › Billing › Invoices. To have a VAT or tax ID, a purchase order number or a company address printed on invoices, add them to the Billing profile before the invoice is issued. An invoice cannot be edited once it has been issued; if something is wrong, request a corrected invoice from billing@northwind.app within 30 days of the invoice date.
