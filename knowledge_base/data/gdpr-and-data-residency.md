# GDPR and data residency

Northwind processes personal data under a Data Processing Addendum that any customer can request from privacy@northwind.app. By default, workspace data is stored in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Business and Enterprise workspaces can choose EU data residency (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt) at the moment the workspace is created. Residency cannot be changed for an existing workspace; to move to the EU region you create a new workspace in the EU and import your data. Our current list of subprocessors is published on the Trust page and updated with 30 days' notice.
