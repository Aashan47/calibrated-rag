# Exporting your data

Owners and Admins can export an entire workspace under Settings › Data › Export. Choose JSON (complete, including comments and history) or CSV (one file per project, tasks only). The export is prepared in the background; you receive an email with a download link within 24 hours, and the link stays valid for 7 days. Attachments are included up to a total of 50 GB; for larger workspaces, contact support@northwind.app to arrange a bulk transfer. Individual projects can also be exported to CSV from the project menu at any time.
