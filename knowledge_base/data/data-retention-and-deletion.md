# Data retention and deletion

Deleted projects, tasks and files are moved to Trash, where they can be restored for 30 days. After 30 days they are permanently deleted from the live service. Encrypted backups are retained for 35 days and then overwritten, so a permanently deleted item is gone from backups within 65 days of the original deletion. Enterprise workspaces can set a custom retention period for Trash between 7 and 365 days.
